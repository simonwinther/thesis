## Limitations and interpretation caveats
1. **Two last digits are genuinely uncertain.** These come from the rounded older per-case text. Gaussian BS1 σ0.5 SSIM is shown as 0.2104, but the computed value is 0.2103505, only 4.6e-7 from the rounding boundary. Gaussian BS1 σ0 PSNR is shown as 13.52, with the computed 13.5249 about 9e-5 from the boundary. A full-precision value could read 0.2103 or 13.53. `verification.json` records this, but the figure does not. Neither cell is a column best.
2. **The σ1.5 BS1 bests are provisional.** That column's outline compares only 3 of 6 rows (Mean, Gaussian, MLP 72 Uniform).
3. **Some bests are very close.** SSIM σ0.5 BS4/2.5k differs by 0.0007, and PSNR σ1.5 BS4/2.5k by 0.009. With one seed, the outlines are descriptive only.
4. **The BS4/2.5k columns at σ0 and σ0.5 mix two kinds of run.** Mean and MLP 72 are standalone 2.5k runs, while Gaussian and MLP 8 are 2.5k checkpoints from 10k runs. I found no learning-rate schedule in `train_wfm.py`, so the settings are equivalent, but the runs are not identical.
5. **The 8 older cells have weaker records.** There is no `commands.json`. Evaluation seed 42 comes from `run_plan.json`, while the other settings come from the logs. They also ran on different hardware (UCloud B200).
6. **Identity rests on logs and commands, not the checkpoints themselves.** Checkpoints are not stored locally, so I could not inspect them.
7. **The PDF text check is by sequence.** I did not parse PDF text coordinates; position consistency rests on the identical sequence and the raster comparison.
