**Medium**

1. **`checks.py:65-68` — gradient check misses the stochastic/KL path.** It only covers `model.encoder` and `model.decoder`. `quant_conv_mu`, `quant_conv_log_sigma` and `post_quant_conv` are top-level `AutoencoderKL` modules, so they are unchecked. `quant_conv_log_sigma` is the only parameter whose gradient exists solely through sampling and KL, so the current assertions would pass unchanged with `deterministic=True`. Fix: assert finite gradients over `model.parameters()` and a nonzero gradient on `model.quant_conv_log_sigma`.
