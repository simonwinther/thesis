**Verdict: accept with caveats.** I found no blocking correctness issue in v2. One clarification is worth making before you present it (see Budget, last point).

**Your two disagreements: you are right on both**

1. **Not a ceiling.** I overstated this in my first review. The encoder is amortised, so `decode(encode(x).mu)` is not even the decoder's best reconstruction of `x`, and a conditional model can land on a better `z`. The accurate label is "oracle-latent reference": it is what a latent model that hit its training target `encode(x_target).mu` exactly would decode to. That makes poor reconstruction a strong practical warning and nothing more. The wording in §5 and §7 is correct; keep it.

2. **Overfit failure is not a capacity proof.** With a 20-minute bound, failure can be time, optimisation or a bug, and even a plateau would not prove a bottleneck limit. §4D says this correctly. My "first change is HF channels" was a suggested order of diagnosis and does not belong in the plan. One thing to know: D trains with `z=mu`, so it never runs the sampling/KL path. Only check A covers that path before the pilot.

**PyWavelets: sensible and minimal**

- It is already a baseline dependency (`DWT_IDWT_layer.py:8`), so nothing new is installed for the comparison.
- From my reading of `DWT_IDWT_Functions.py:122-135`, the baseline's band letters follow the three spatial axes in order. That is the same convention as `pywt.dwtn` keys `aaa…ddd` with `axes=(-3,-2,-1)`.
- The Haar high-pass sign should also agree, at (x[2i] − x[2i+1])/√2 in both. This is derived from the source and my memory of the PyWavelets docs, not run, so your equivalence check stays the gate.
- All dims are even and the filter has length 2, so the boundary mode should not matter.

Two practical caveats for the check:
- The baseline sizes its matrices from the last two dims only (`DWT_IDWT_layer.py:465`). Small non-cubic test tensors must keep the first spatial dim ≤ the larger of the other two, or the reference itself breaks. The real 224×224×160 shape is fine.
- Pass float32 explicitly so both sides compute in the same precision.
